1. Executive Summary
This report presents a comprehensive analysis of delivery operations of a global e-commerce company managing end-to-end order fullfillment across multiple regions. The analysis croves 1,72,765 orders spanning Jan 2015 through Jan 2018, focusing on identifying root causes of chronic late deliveries, quantifying their financial impact, and establisheding a data-driven framework for improvement

The headline finding are shocking i.e : 54.71% of all orders are delivered late, costing the Business $2.1M in eroded Profitability on delayed orders alone. While total profit across fullfilled orders reached $7.5M, the persistent late-delivery problem represens both a significant financial drain and a major customer experience risk.

KEY FINDINGS :
overall late delivery rate : 54.71%
on time delivery rate : 45.29%
total orders analyzed : 1,72,765
total profit (Profitable orders) : $7.5M
profit at risk(delayed orders) : $2.1M

2. Business Context and Objectives
The company operates a gloabl e-commerce platform selling productss across categories including sporting goods, fitness equipment, outdoor gear, footwear, and apparel across multiple international regions

CORE PROBLEM :
Actual shipping times frequently deviate from scheduled delivery windows, creating eroded customer trust reduced order profitability and an inability to make reliable commitments to buyers at point of purchase


ANALYTICAL OBJECTIVES :
1. Understand the current state of delivery performance across all dimensions (region, mode, time, segment)
2. Quantify the financial impact of delays an order profitability
3. Identify the primary operational bottlenecks driving late deliveries
4. Build a predictive model to flag high-risk orders before they are shipped (advanced)
5. Deliver actionable recommendations to improve on-time delivery and profitability

Figure : delay_percentage_by_time

8. Strategic Recommendations
Based on the findings across all analytical dimensions, the following priortized actions are recommendations :
1. CRITIAL - immediately audit first class & second class shipping capacity : first class shipping has a 100% delay rate and second class 79.8%, these modes operates in complete contradiction to their brand promise, conduct an immediate carrier SLA audit. Until resolved, consider suspending the first class and repricing to reflect actual performance
2. HIGH - Resolve payment processsing bottlenecks : Orders in PAYMENT_REVIEW (55.3% overall, 80% in central africa) and PENDING_PAYMENT(55%) have significantly elevated delay rates. Introduce automcated escalation for stalled payment reviews exceeding 2 hours
3. MEDIUM - Develop seasonal surge capacity plans : august, october(5.4%), december(55.2%) are peak delay months. Build pre-season capacity plans including temporary logistics partnerships, inventorypre-positioning, and adjusted delivery promise windows
4. MEDIUM - Default to standard class for eligible orders : standard class achieves 39.8% delay rate, for superior to first and second class. re-evaluate shipping mode assignment logic to default eligible orders to standard class, particularly in regions and departments with poor premium mode performance
5. MEDIUM - outdoors(61.3%) and golf(90.8%) in central africa show significantly elevated delay rates. conduct a warehouse-level audit of stock availability pick and pack times, and carrier assignment for these product categories.
6. LOW - reduce loss making order share (18.7%) : these orders warrants a seprate pricing and discount review. combine with delay Analysis to Identify wheather discounted orders are disproportationtely late creating a compounded margin problem
